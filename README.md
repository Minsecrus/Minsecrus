In this intricate and complicated world, what I want to do is simple:  
在纷繁复杂的世界里，我想做的事很简单：  
***Practice game theory and fitting, and listen to wishes and cries.***  
***践行博弈与拟合，倾听愿望与呐喊。***  
This is especially true in this age of great convergence.  
在大融合的时代，尤其如此。

---

Rather than chasing ever-changing tools, I would rather explore values that endure a little longer.  
比起追逐不断变化的工具，我更愿探索相对恒久的价值。

Rather than getting tangled in many different specialties, I would rather uncover abilities that are more broadly applicable.  
比起纠结多样的专业，我更愿挖掘相对通用的能力。

I deeply love mathematics and physics: they tell me that intuition and logic are not mutually exclusive, and together they contain a silent, profound beauty.  
我深爱着数学和物理：她们告诉我，直觉和逻辑不互斥，二者共同蕴含着无言大美。

I do not study computer science, but I know well the value of computer-related science and technology in our time. I am willing to compute the world with greater precision and reconstruct it through automation.  
我不学计算机，但我深知计算机相关的科学与技术在当今时代的价值。我愿用更高的精度计算世界，用自动化的方式重构世界。

I enjoy reading, especially the classics. Perhaps there is a trace of affectation in that. I am grateful for every book I have read, because they let me see the reverse side of the world without having to live through it myself.  
我喜欢阅读，尤其经典著作。可能有些附庸风雅的嫌疑吧。感谢所有我读过的书，它们让我无须亲身经历，就能看到世界的背面。

I love reciting poetry, and sometimes I enthusiastically write poems as well. But living in modern society, I do not have the leisurely elegance of old scholars, so I can only stir words in my spare time. Still, I will not sigh in vain that "writing poems and composing prose by the north window, ten thousand words are not worth a cup of water," because when words begin to flow, thoughts and ideas drift with them.  
我喜欢吟诗，有时还兴致勃勃地写诗。但生活在现代社会的我，没有文人高士的闲情逸致，只能在闲暇里将文字撩拨。但我不会空叹“
吟诗作赋北窗里，万言不直一杯水”，因为文字流动时，思绪和思想也会随之飘飞。

---

Some people may care about my age, but that is not important. When I realize that some people younger than I am possess greater technical ability and see the world more deeply, the resolve to strive harder may be the only real meaning of comparing ages. I sincerely wish those who achieve much while young an ever-broadening road ahead.  
也许有的人想关注我的年龄，但这不重要。发觉某些比我年龄小的人技术力比我高，看世界比我深刻时，生发一些奋发图强的决心，可能是年龄攀比的唯一意义。我由衷祝福那些年少有为的人，希望他们的路越走越宽。

It has been more than six years since I first came into contact with programming. I often feel that time waits for no one.  
我接触编程到现在已经六年多了。大有时不我待之感。

Do not drift along with time, or dreams will decay; while the moonlight has not yet gone far, use it to brew wine.  
别顺着时光漂流，梦会腐朽；趁月光没远走，拿它泡酒。

---

I am grateful to my parents. The trunk bears all the weight, while the leaves drift only for themselves.  
感谢我的父母。树干承载着全部的重量，叶子却只为自己飘扬。
